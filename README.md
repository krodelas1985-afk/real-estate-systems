# Real Estate Systems

Create a polished, responsive personal portfolio and resume website for:

Kathy Rodelas Talabis
Real Estate Operations, CRM & Workflow Automation Specialist

The website’s purpose is to attract high-paying remote employment, freelance clients, and business partnerships in real estate operations, CRM management, lead nurturing, and workflow automation.

This should not look like a generic virtual assistant portfolio or a software developer portfolio. It should position Kathy as a real estate operations professional who understands the daily work of agents and can also build practical systems and automations to improve it.

Overall message

Kathy has worked in real estate operations since 2017. Her experience includes lead generation, buyer and seller follow-up, CRM management, appointment setting, off-market and expired-property outreach, real estate research, database organization, campaign support, and workflow automation.

She is also the founder of BaMo, a Philippine real estate technology startup that combines a property marketplace, campaign management, CRM workflows, AI-assisted conversations, lead qualification, follow-up, and agent support.

The visitor should understand within the first 10 seconds that Kathy can:

Work directly with real estate leads and clients

Manage and organize a real estate CRM

Improve follow-up systems

Identify operational problems

Design practical automations

Connect real estate knowledge with AI and technology

Problem

Real estate leads were not always receiving consistent follow-up, especially when agents were busy with calls, viewings, and other clients.

System

A workflow was created to review lead activity, decide whether follow-up was appropriate, send controlled follow-up messages, escalate important conversations, and stop when a lead opted out or completed the process.

Early activity

62 distinct leads followed up

154 follow-up messages sent

25.8% of contacted leads replied

Escalation and stopping rules included

Kathy’s role

Workflow planning, CRM logic, prompt development, testing, performance monitoring, and process improvement.
